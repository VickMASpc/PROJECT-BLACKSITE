# Adult Content Systems

This document exists so BLACKSITE's adult content remains systemic and contextual instead of degenerating into a folder of unrelated animations.

## 1. Persistent compromise

The basic pattern:

\`decision -> physical/social state -> movement/dialogue change -> later complication -> new command\`

A compromised state can affect:

- walk speed
- sprint availability
- crouch/crawl
- sitting
- ladders/vents
- inspections
- disguises
- guard suspicion
- camera posture
- inventory access
- dialogue cadence
- available commands

A scene is valuable when it gives the rest of the mission more material.

## 2. S-Machines

**S-Machines** are the working category for facility machinery or mission devices built around sustained adult interaction rather than a single animation.

They must be state machines, not "play animation and wait."

Core parameters:

- profile/type
- intensity
- rhythm/cycle
- duration
- body position/posture
- restraint requirements
- ability to interrupt
- publicness / witness risk
- mission purpose
- accumulated load
- aftereffects

Possible roles in missions:

- experimental equipment the facility actually uses
- a device that must be endured to maintain cover
- interrogation/training infrastructure
- access-control hardware with obscene requirements
- a machine the player can deliberately keep running longer for another tactical benefit
- a damaged machine that becomes unpredictable
- a device whose state follows Reina after release through strain/recovery debt

The interesting question is never only "what does the machine do?" It is "what does surviving it do to the next room?"

## 3. Machine endurance

Endurance is not HP.

A machine interaction consumes capacity based on:

\`load = intensity x duration x posture factor x existing strain x context modifier\`

The first implementation can be simpler, but the conceptual separation matters:

- **Endurance** falls during sustained load.
- **Strain** accumulates and makes future load more expensive.
- **Recovery debt** means leaving the machine does not instantly reset Reina.
- **Composure** controls outward performance under observation.
- **Discomfort** controls movement/logistical interference afterward.

Low endurance should alter:

- movement speed
- idle posture
- speech length
- willingness to accept another endurance order
- animation stability
- recovery time
- tactical options

Hard stops are real. Reina can reject a physically impossible order.

That is better game design than infinite compliance.

## 4. B/D layer

B/D is not a separate "mode." It is a command/relationship layer that can intersect normal stealth.

It can include:

- restraint states
- positional orders
- enforced waiting/holding positions
- discipline/penalty structures
- command-language escalation
- authority play between commander and subordinate
- using restraint as part of disguise, cover, transport, access, or facility procedure

The important BLACKSITE twist is that B/D states should create **operational consequences**.

A restrained Reina may be easier to pass through one area and much harder to extract from another. A positional order can solve a camera line while creating exposure if a guard rounds the corner.

## 5. Humiliation

Humiliation should come from specificity and consequences, not a generic EMBARRASSMENT +10.

Useful dimensions:

- who can see
- whether Reina is in uniform
- whether she has to keep a professional face
- whether a guard/scientist believes the situation is routine
- whether the player caused it
- whether Reina had to suggest it
- whether evidence/logs remain
- whether the state continues after the immediate danger
- whether it happens again in a worse context
- whether the two characters have become frighteningly practiced at it

The strongest humiliating situations are often mechanically mundane afterward:

- she has to walk through a normal hallway
- answer a security question
- ride an elevator
- crawl a vent
- stand still for a scan
- maintain eye contact with someone who has no idea what is happening

## 6. Normalization

Normalization is intentionally hidden-ish.

Do not put a giant "DEPRAVITY 82%" bar on screen.

It should change:

- which lines Reina uses
- how quickly she recognizes the ugly solution
- whether she prepares without being asked
- whether she proposes a questionable option
- whether the commander can still surprise her
- how much procedural shorthand exists between them

## 7. No instant reset

After adult content:

- clothes may remain changed
- props may remain attached
- locomotion may remain altered
- endurance may remain reduced
- strain may remain elevated
- a facility log may remain dirty
- a witness may remember
- Reina's opinion of the commander's handling may change
- the next scene may inherit the whole mess

That is BLACKSITE.
