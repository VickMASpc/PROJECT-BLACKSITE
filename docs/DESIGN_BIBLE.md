# PROJECT BLACKSITE — Design Bible

Status: **G0 / active planning**

This document is authoritative for current creative direction.

The unfinished \`Prison Academia.md\` transcription is a tonal/structural reference, not a plot or character template. BLACKSITE should capture the **scene-construction intelligence** behind that material without copying its cast or scenario.

---

# 1. Core fantasy

BLACKSITE is a first-person infiltration game living inside Figura.

The player operates with one adult subordinate field operative, **Reina Kurose**, inside highly controlled facilities where military/security procedure, experimental technology, personal authority, and sexual humiliation can collide.

The game takes its espionage seriously.

The game also allows situations to become deeply indecent without suddenly switching into a separate "porn mode."

The fantasy is not simply:

> command an attractive subordinate to do sexual things

It is:

> know Reina well enough, control enough of the mission, and understand enough of the procedure to turn one compromised situation into an escalating social and logistical trap — while both characters continue trying to function as professionals.

---

# 2. The commander

## 2.1 Role

The player is Reina's operational superior and mission handler in the field.

The Commander is not a silent neutral camera.

The player role permits:

- legitimate tactical leadership
- teasing
- opportunism
- deliberate unfairness
- procedural pedantry
- information asymmetry
- choosing the dirtier option even when a cleaner one exists
- prolonging an embarrassing situation because the opportunity is too good
- asking inconvenient questions at the worst possible time
- exploiting Reina's own standards, wording, or professionalism in dialogue

This is important.

If every compromising order is the only objectively correct tactical solution, the Commander becomes morally and erotically boring.

Sometimes there should be a cleaner route.

Sometimes the player should knowingly choose the more humiliating route.

Reina may realize this immediately, halfway through, or only afterward.

## 2.2 Authority is not mind control

Reina is subordinate, not an empty puppet.

She may:

- object
- argue
- demand justification
- identify a better tactical option
- comply while furious
- comply because the logic is defensible
- negotiate conditions
- refuse something physically impossible
- later discover that the Commander withheld a cleaner alternative
- eventually pre-empt the Commander's reasoning because she knows exactly what kind of bastard she is working with

The relationship becomes interesting because the Commander has authority **and Reina has a mind**.

# 3. Player/Reina history

This relationship must predate Operation Glasshouse.

Current starting canon:

- Reina has already completed multiple operations under the Commander.
- They trust each other's field competence.
- She knows the Commander can be manipulative and enjoys exploiting technicalities.
- The Commander knows Reina's pride, habits, euphemisms, and professional weak points.
- Their prior missions have contained awkward or boundary-testing moments, but Glasshouse is where the partnership begins crossing into sustained, mission-integrated sexual absurdity.
- Reina does not begin as sexually naive. What catches her is not the existence of sex; it is being made to preserve professional dignity while the situation becomes indefensible.
- They are familiar enough that the Commander can ask personal questions with history behind them.
- Reina is familiar enough with the Commander to recognize certain looks, pauses, or suspiciously formal phrasing before an order is even spoken.

Important tonal target:

> Reina sees the Commander's expression.
>
> "No."
>
> The Commander has not said anything yet.
>
> "Whatever justification you are constructing, no."

That only works if they already know each other.

# 4. Reina's fundamental contradiction

Reina's erotic/comedic value does **not** come from simply being shy.

She is proud, intelligent, competent, disciplined, and extremely invested in maintaining professional control.

The scene gets stronger when those traits remain intact.

The game should repeatedly put her in situations where:

- her body/state is compromising
- the procedure is ridiculous
- the Commander is enjoying this far too much
- and she still tries to speak, reason, report, negotiate, or act like an operative

Her dignity is not removed.

It is continuously rebuilt and challenged.

This creates rhythm:

\`loss of control -> recovery of professionalism -> new trap -> recovery -> worse trap\`

A long BLACKSITE scene should often contain several of these cycles.

# 5. Scene grammar

This is the single most important lesson taken from the Prison Academia reference.

BLACKSITE scenes should be **deep before they are numerous**.

A physical premise that could be summarized in one sentence may support a long scene if the interpersonal context keeps changing.

## 5.1 Basic escalation ladder

A strong scene can move through:

1. **Practical problem**
   - something must be removed, concealed, endured, tested, recorded, transported, or passed through security.

2. **Professional framing**
   - procedure, report, inspection, cover identity, experiment protocol, chain of command.

3. **First concession**
   - Reina agrees because the justification is legitimate or technically defensible.

4. **Documentation / observation**
   - camera, report, witness, sensor, medical/scientific record, verbal confirmation.

5. **Precision trap**
   - Reina uses a euphemism.
   - Commander demands precise language because the report "requires accuracy."

6. **Personal tangent**
   - the Commander exploits the fact that Reina cannot conveniently leave.
   - asks something unrelated but revealing.
   - Reina immediately realizes this is the worst possible time for that conversation.

7. **Rule stretching**
   - a request begins plausible and becomes more openly opportunistic.

8. **Reina reasserts dignity**
   - corrects wording, resumes report, straightens posture, returns to procedure.

9. **Physical/social escalation**
   - the original problem becomes harder, more public, more prolonged, or more difficult to hide.

10. **Accidental honesty**
    - under pressure, Reina says something more revealing than intended.

11. **Retraction**
    - she recognizes what she said and tries to formally correct the record.

12. **Commander weaponizes the correction**
    - another semantic or procedural trap.

13. **Completion that is not really completion**
    - the immediate task ends, but the state, evidence, mess, fatigue, outfit, witness, device, or humiliation continues.

This ladder is not mandatory in every scene. It is a toolbox.

# 6. Dialogue is gameplay

BLACKSITE must not treat dialogue as decoration around animations.

Speech can be an active pressure mechanic.

Examples of interaction patterns:

- **report**
- **clarify**
- **repeat**
- **define**
- **confirm**
- **correct the record**
- **state condition**
- **acknowledge order**
- **continue despite interruption**
- **answer unrelated question**
- **maintain cover while compromised**

The Commander can exploit institutional language.

Reina might say:

> "The apparatus is causing significant interference."

The Commander can ask:

> "Define interference for the report."

That is a mechanically meaningful escalation even before anything physical changes.

## 6.1 Composure as behavior, not a bar

Composure should affect:

- sentence length
- vocabulary precision
- whether Reina defaults to euphemism
- whether she can maintain cover
- whether she corrects herself
- pauses
- gaze
- posture
- whether she can complete a formal report without assistance

Do not reduce this to "COMPOSURE 63%" on the HUD.

# 7. The one-step-lower rule

Every important scene asks what is underneath the obvious payoff.

For any idea, ask:

- What remains physically true afterward?
- What evidence remains?
- What does Reina still have to carry/wear/endure?
- Does she have to walk through a normal hallway immediately afterward?
- Can she sit?
- Can she crawl?
- Can she climb?
- Can she maintain cover?
- Who saw?
- Who understood what they saw?
- Did someone misunderstand it as normal procedure?
- Did the Commander record it?
- Did Reina have to describe it herself?
- Did she accidentally reveal something personal?
- Can another checkpoint make the exact same state relevant again?
- Does fatigue or bodily mess create a later problem?
- Does Reina discover the Commander could have ended it earlier?
- Does the Commander learn a new button to press next time?
- Does Reina become more prepared for that button next time?

The aftermath is part of the scene.

# 8. Humiliation model

Humiliation is contextual, not a generic meter.

Useful dimensions:

## Audience
- private with Commander
- anonymous technician
- known colleague
- guard
- scientist
- multiple witnesses
- camera-only
- recorded for later review

## Understanding
- observer knows exactly what is happening
- observer sees something but misreads it
- observer treats it as routine facility procedure
- observer is professionally indifferent
- observer is deliberately humiliating

## Reina's role
- ordered
- technically volunteered
- suggested the option herself
- forced by circumstances
- tricked by incomplete information
- knowingly chose the dirty route
- realizes too late that a clean route existed

## Professional contradiction
- in uniform
- giving a report
- maintaining cover identity
- giving orders to someone else
- answering security questions
- standing inspection
- trying to discuss serious mission information

## Persistence
- visual evidence
- facility log
- recording
- damaged/altered clothing
- compromised movement
- bodily accident/mess
- witness memory
- item/device still present
- future callback

A bodily mishap such as losing bladder control can be useful **when it emerges from prolonged strain, failed composure, or a badly timed climax of the situation**, not as random decoration. The key is what happens next: she still has to finish the report, cross the room, meet someone, or live with the evidence.

# 9. Sexual content should have different flavors

Not every adult scene should chase maximum physical intensity.

BLACKSITE should distinguish:

## Procedural
Erotic because an institutional process requires intimate compliance.

## Command-driven
Erotic because the Commander directs Reina and she reacts to the authority dynamic.

## Humiliating
Erotic because of observation, language, exposure, contradiction, or evidence.

## Endurance
Erotic because Reina has to sustain something while continuing the mission.

## Opportunistic
Erotic because the Commander notices a chance that is not strictly necessary.

## Self-incriminating
Erotic because Reina herself has to describe, admit, request, or propose something.

## Normalized
Erotic because both characters treat something outrageous as routine after repeated exposure.

A scene can combine several, but they should not all feel identical.

# 10. S-Machine principle — first pass

S-Machines are not random sex toys scattered through a dungeon.

They are a family of facility systems that can be justified by the world: conditioning, biometric testing, interrogation, behavioral research, performance testing, access/security, medical experimentation, or human-factors research.

An S-Machine scene should be capable of producing:

- a professional reason to use it
- a reason the Commander controls settings or procedure
- a reason Reina must communicate during it
- sustained rather than instant pressure
- observable performance
- data/reporting
- endurance cost
- aftereffects
- a later callback

The best S-Machine is not the one with the most explicit animation.

It is the one that creates the most **scene material**.

Detailed categories will be designed later in G0.

# 11. B/D and control — first pass

B/D should be represented as **control states**, not merely bondage animations.

Potential state families:

- free
- ordered posture
- hold-position command
- escorted
- partially secured
- fully secured
- transport restraint
- procedural restraint
- machine restraint

Each state must change gameplay:

- movement
- interaction options
- cover plausibility
- detection risk
- ability to escape
- ability to use equipment
- whether Reina needs the Commander for release
- how humiliating a public interaction becomes

The core question is always:

> What does this state make easier now, and harder five minutes later?

# 12. First design target for G1

None of the erotic systems above are implemented in G1.

G1 exists to make Reina herself good enough to deserve them.

Before facility content resumes, Reina must be enjoyable to:

- summon
- walk beside
- leave waiting
- call over
- direct to a nearby position
- watch idle
- see turn toward the player
- move through basic Minecraft terrain
- recover if navigation fails
- interact with through one clean targeting method

That is the next implementation target **after G0 is complete**.
