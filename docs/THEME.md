# PROJECT BLACKSITE — Theme and Creative North Star

## Elevator pitch

**Metal Gear-style infiltration with the logic of an obscure Japanese eroge slowly infecting the mission.**

The player is inside a serious, sealed research facility with one highly competent adult subordinate operative. Security procedures, restricted areas, cover identities, surveillance, extraction routes, and the facility's actual research are played straight.

Then mission logic begins producing absurdly indecent practical problems.

The game does **not** constantly wink at the player. The more professionally everyone treats a ridiculous situation, the better the BLACKSITE tone works.

The specific inspiration that started the project was the kind of scene in *Prison Academia* where an already ridiculous erotic problem becomes a logistical problem the characters still have to solve. BLACKSITE takes that energy into stealth/infiltration rather than copying that game's plot or characters.

## Core tone

The target combination is:

- competent espionage
- claustrophobic facility exploration
- deadpan character humor
- adult erotic absurdity
- humiliation caused by **context**, not random slapstick
- persistent aftermath
- occasional serious horror when the facility itself stops being funny

The project must be able to move from:

> "Camera cycling. Move in eight seconds."

to:

> "Commander. I am requesting—formally—that we do not sprint."

and later into a genuinely grim laboratory discovery without feeling like three unrelated games.

## The one-step-lower rule

Whenever an idea works, BLACKSITE asks what happens **after** the obvious payoff.

A scene is not complete because the animation ended.

Ask:

1. What is still physically true five minutes later?
2. What new action is harder because of it?
3. Can the next security procedure make it relevant again?
4. Does Reina walk, sit, crawl, speak, or behave differently afterward?
5. Does anyone else notice?
6. Can the player choose to accommodate her, exploit the state, or make it worse?
7. Can the same problem recur after the player assumed it was over?
8. What does repetition normalize between commander and subordinate?

This is the main anti-"animation gallery" rule.

## Relationship arc

Reina is not meant to become a generic lust meter.

Her progression is about **normalization**.

Early:

> "Absolutely not."

Then:

> "There has to be another way."

Later:

> "...How long do I have?"

Eventually the partnership can become so operationally warped that Reina anticipates the command before the player gives it.

The endpoint is not "she becomes stupidly horny." The endpoint is two professionals acquiring an utterly broken shared definition of **standard operating procedure**.

## Adult dynamic and agency

All principal erotic characters are adults.

Reina is subordinate in the chain of command, and the command dynamic is supposed to matter. She can argue, object, negotiate, complain, comply, and sometimes propose the indecent option herself.

BLACKSITE's adult command content operates inside an established mission/relationship protocol rather than treating inability to refuse as erotic content. Reina retains hard stops. Mechanically, this is useful too: endurance and context can make a command unavailable, and a refusal can itself create a tactical problem.

That preserves the desired commander/subordinate tension without making her a mindless prop.

## What BLACKSITE is not

- not Cynthia with 40 more animation buttons
- not a generic romance simulator
- not a stat spreadsheet pretending to be intimacy
- not a nonstop comedy where guards and scientists behave like sitcom characters
- not a sequence of erotic scenes that reset Reina to default afterward
- not a conventional Minecraft companion following at a fixed distance with no awareness

The companion, the world, and the mission state must continuously contaminate each other.
