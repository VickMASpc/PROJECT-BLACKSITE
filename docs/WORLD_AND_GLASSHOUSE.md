# WORLD + OPERATION GLASSHOUSE — First Canon Draft

Status: **G0 draft, not fully locked**

This document gives BLACKSITE a concrete world and first-operation premise so later planning is not floating in abstraction.

---

# 1. Setting

Near-future, grounded military/biotech espionage.

BLACKSITE should feel technologically advanced enough to justify experimental surveillance, body-integrated devices, behavioral conditioning, biometric security, and bizarre human-factors research, but not so futuristic that everything becomes magic.

Weapons, access systems, labs, uniforms, security rooms, cameras, service corridors, elevators, maintenance shafts, server rooms, and transport infrastructure should still feel physical and industrial.

The erotic absurdity works better when the surrounding world looks credible.

---

# 2. The player's organization

Working name:

**Strategic Recovery Directorate — SRD**

A multinational covert organization specializing in:

- recovery of stolen military research
- blacksite infiltration
- compromised technology retrieval
- counter-proliferation
- covert extraction of personnel and data
- operations where conventional military presence would cause political fallout

The SRD is not clean.

It uses people like Reina because certain facilities cannot be raided publicly.

It also has enough compartmentalization that field teams do not always know what their own side has funded.

That becomes important in Glasshouse.

---

# 3. The Commander

Working title:

**Field Controller**

The player is physically present in the operation rather than sitting in a remote command room.

The Field Controller's responsibilities include:

- mission authority
- access to classified technical information
- operating specialist equipment
- overriding experimental devices
- making route decisions
- authorizing cover procedures
- maintaining mission records
- deciding what can be abandoned and what must be recovered

This naturally creates information asymmetry with Reina.

The Commander sometimes genuinely knows things she does not.

That makes it possible to create scenes where Reina cannot immediately tell whether an ugly instruction is:

- genuinely required
- only the safest option
- technically defensible but unnecessary
- or the Commander being a bastard

The player should not always be told which category it is.

---

# 4. Reina's role

Working rank:

**Captain Reina Kurose**

Role:

**Field Infiltration Specialist**

Her specialties:

- restricted-area infiltration
- surveillance avoidance
- close-quarters movement
- disguise/cover discipline
- tactical reconnaissance
- physical retrieval
- maintaining cover under interrogation or inspection
- operating inside facilities where overt force would compromise the mission

She is not the Commander's secretary, assistant, or disposable subordinate.

She is the person trusted to go physically deeper into a blacksite than almost anyone else.

That makes humiliating or compromised states hit harder because they are happening to someone whose baseline identity is competence.

---

# 5. Why Reina works with this Commander

They have completed several operations together.

The relationship is built on an uncomfortable fact:

**the Commander gets results.**

Reina has seen the player:

- improvise solutions that should not have worked
- exploit regulations and technicalities
- withhold information until the exact moment it becomes useful
- push her into awkward positions for legitimate tactical reasons
- occasionally enjoy those positions far too much
- save her life when the joking stops

She trusts the Commander's competence more than she trusts the Commander's sense of mercy.

That is the foundation.

The Commander, in turn, knows that Reina will challenge bullshit but will also follow a plan if she cannot break the reasoning.

That creates the exact conversational battleground BLACKSITE needs.

---

# 6. Prior operation — working history

The mission immediately before Glasshouse is tentatively called:

**Operation WHITE NOISE**

The pair infiltrated a diplomatic data relay disguised as a private medical contractor.

Reina had to maintain a clinical cover identity while the Commander manipulated inspection rules, equipment declarations, and medical paperwork to keep them inside.

Nothing fully crossed into the sustained sexual absurdity Glasshouse will introduce, but the operation established:

- Reina hates being trapped by procedural logic
- the Commander knows exactly how formal she becomes when embarrassed
- Reina has already said some version of "you could have told me that earlier"
- the Commander has already proven willing to exploit technical truths
- both know the other is capable of continuing a mission through personally humiliating circumstances

This gives Glasshouse continuity without making it their first awkward day together.

# 7. The facility

Working name:

**Asterion Human Systems Annex 6**

SRD codename:

**GLASSHOUSE**

Public cover:

A privately operated human-performance and medical-device research facility specializing in:

- occupational ergonomics
- high-stress work environments
- biosensor development
- rehabilitation hardware
- neural/physiological monitoring
- long-duration equipment testing

Actual work:

- interrogation-resistance conditioning
- covert-device carriage
- biometric deception
- sensory overload studies
- involuntary-response measurement
- restraint tolerance
- human-machine compliance research
- internal and body-adjacent covert transport systems
- behavioral conditioning under observation
- procedures intended to determine how much humiliation, discomfort, fatigue, and physiological interference an operative can endure while still appearing functional

This is why BLACKSITE's strangest machinery belongs there.

The facility is not secretly a fetish club.

It is worse in a different way:

**it has clinically rationalized things that become erotic, invasive, or humiliating in practice.**

Scientists may discuss something outrageous with complete professional indifference.

That is ideal BLACKSITE tone.

---

# 8. S-Machines — in-world meaning

Working formal name:

**Somatic Stress Systems**

Staff slang:

**S-Machines**

They are modular human-performance rigs used to impose and measure controlled physiological or behavioral stress while the subject performs another task.

The key word is **simultaneous**.

An S-Machine is rarely testing only endurance.

It may require the subject to:

- answer questions
- read technical material
- maintain posture
- identify visual targets
- operate a console
- preserve a cover story
- report symptoms precisely
- remain silent
- continue despite escalating interference

This immediately creates the BLACKSITE scene structure where dialogue and professionalism matter as much as the machine.

---

# 9. The first compromised-device family

Working name:

**MULE capsules**

Purpose:

Experimental covert data carriers intended to bypass conventional scanners and external equipment searches.

Properties under consideration:

- small sealed metal memory capsules
- body-carried rather than stored in visible equipment
- active anti-scan oscillation
- a retention/locking mechanism
- remote diagnostic/control access
- removal procedure that can be made easier or harder depending on device state
- repeated capsules rather than one object, allowing prolonged retrieval/discharge rather than instant resolution
- physiological side effects caused by prolonged active operation
- recorded testing procedures because the facility cares about "usability"

This deliberately captures what is compelling about the reference scene's device concept without copying its exact fiction.

Important design principle:

The capsules are interesting because of **everything around removal**:

- who has control
- how much Reina knows
- whether the Commander is fully honest about removal requirements
- whether the process must be documented
- whether Reina is required to provide a usability report
- how long it takes
- what accidental admissions happen under strain
- whether a bodily accident creates additional humiliation
- whether there are still capsules left afterward

The device is a scene engine, not just a prop.

# 10. Why GLASSHOUSE is being infiltrated

Initial SRD intelligence says Annex 6 contains:

**a stolen adaptive biometric key system** capable of teaching security hardware to distinguish between authentic and spoofed human responses.

If deployed widely, it would make many current SRD infiltration techniques obsolete.

The official objectives are:

1. enter Annex 6 without revealing SRD involvement
2. obtain the adaptive key dataset
3. identify who purchased the research
4. leave without triggering a facility-wide purge

The site cannot simply be destroyed because:

- the buyers are unknown
- destroying it would warn them
- the dataset may already exist elsewhere
- SRD needs proof of the chain of custody
- there may be personnel worth extracting
- the facility's network is heavily segmented and air-gapped

This forces a physical infiltration.

---

# 11. The Glasshouse twist

Midway through the operation, Reina and the Commander discover:

**SRD procurement signatures inside the research archive.**

Not necessarily proof that the entire organization approved the work.

But proof that someone on their side:

- funded part of it
- supplied test parameters
- or requested specific human-performance research

The mission changes.

It is no longer merely:

> steal enemy research

It becomes:

> determine who inside your own organization knew about this place

This creates the serious tonal pivot the game needs.

The depraved machinery stops being funny for a while because it has suddenly become evidence.

# 12. First-operation structure — high level

Glasshouse should eventually be a long mission, but its structure should be built around a few **deep situations**, not a constant stream of new gimmicks.

Working act structure:

## Act I — Entry

- exterior approach
- service perimeter
- cover establishment
- Reina/Commander banter establishing history
- first security procedure
- first indication that facility protocols are unusually invasive

## Act II — Cover

- staff/service route
- disguise
- inspection procedures
- first MULE-capsule problem begins
- the Commander discovers that the situation can be manipulated
- Reina tries to keep the mission professional

## Act III — The long compromised sequence

This is the first major scene-engine sequence.

It should not be one animation.

It should evolve through:

- practical need
- technical explanation
- formal reporting
- precise symptom questions
- camera/record requirement
- personal tangent
- procedural argument
- Reina attempting to restore dignity
- worsening physical/logistical difficulty
- accidental honesty
- correction/retraction
- partial resolution
- realization that the situation is not fully over

A bodily accident can occur here if earned by the duration/strain and then become **another thing Reina has to carry into the next professional interaction**.

The important part is not the accident itself.

The important part is that the mission does not politely cut away afterward.

## Act IV — Research core

- S-Machine rooms
- records
- human-performance data
- sterile staff behavior
- humor drops away
- SRD connection discovered
- Reina's attitude toward the mission changes

## Act V — Deep Glasshouse

- route changes because original extraction assumptions are now unsafe
- Reina and Commander must use facility procedures they previously mocked
- first major S-Machine sequence
- earlier compromised state may return as a constraint

## Act VI — Extraction

- evidence must physically leave the site
- at least one earlier choice affects extraction
- facility may know that "something" happened without knowing who the infiltrators were
- Reina and Commander leave with unresolved tension about SRD involvement
- some humiliating evidence/log may still exist inside the facility

The mission should end with both:

**plot momentum** and **relationship contamination**.

---

# 13. Tone test

A BLACKSITE scene is probably on target if all of these can be true at once:

- the facility procedure makes internal sense
- Reina has a defensible reason to cooperate
- the Commander has room to make it worse
- Reina can argue intelligently
- dialogue adds pressure rather than pausing the scene
- the situation can become funny without characters becoming stupid
- something remains afterward
- five minutes later, both characters are still dealing with it
