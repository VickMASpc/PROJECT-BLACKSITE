# Reina Kurose

## Role

Adult field operative assigned beneath the player-character's command for Operation Glasshouse.

Reina is the project's first and primary companion. BLACKSITE should become deep around one woman before becoming broad around many interchangeable companions.

## Baseline personality

Reina is:

- highly competent
- disciplined
- dry
- observant
- economical with words
- more likely to become **formal** when embarrassed than loud
- willing to challenge stupid orders
- capable of following an ugly order when the tactical reasoning is sound
- capable of suggesting an ugly solution herself once the partnership becomes normalized

She should never collapse into a squealing anime reaction machine.

Her humor comes from control failing around the edges.

## Speech

Normal:

> "Camera cycling. Move in eight seconds."

Annoyed:

> "That was not stealth. That was surviving the consequences of your decision."

Embarrassed:

> "Commander. I am making a formal request that we walk."

Deep normalization:

> "Storage room. Thirty seconds. Don't say anything."

The funniest late-game reversal is when the player hesitates and Reina has already begun preparing for the awful solution because she knows exactly what the commander is going to ask.

## Behavioral progression

Normalization changes behavior before it changes dialogue.

Low normalization:

- waits for explicit questionable orders
- objects early
- tries conventional solutions first

Medium:

- discusses absurd options clinically
- anticipates familiar complications
- performs small preparations before being asked

High:

- proposes solutions that early Reina would have rejected
- becomes disturbingly efficient at compromised mission states
- may leave the player as the more visibly uncomfortable professional

## Persistent state dimensions

These are not all meant to be visible as bars.

- **Endurance** — remaining ability to sustain physically taxing/compromised states.
- **Strain** — accumulated load that slows recovery and changes movement.
- **Discomfort** — immediate physical/logistical interference.
- **Composure** — how controlled she can remain outwardly.
- **Annoyance** — specifically toward the commander/current handling of the mission.
- **Normalization** — how much BLACKSITE's absurd operating procedure has become ordinary to her.
- **Disguise** — FIELD, STAFF, future identities.
- **Concealment state** — none or context-specific persistent carry states.
- **Machine state** — current S-Machine profile, intensity, duration, load, and recovery debt.

## Visual direction

Final model goals:

- adult woman
- field-operative silhouette first; erotic states should contrast with that competence
- face built for small deadpan changes, not only exaggerated expressions
- clothing split into manipulable groups
- prop attachment anchors designed in from the first bbmodel
- clean first-person visibility rules
- animation groups that do not require giant hierarchy transforms
- posture states for normal / careful / strained / restrained / machine-interaction
- separate disguise variants without replacing the entire model

The v0.1 procedural block proxy is temporary and exists only to test the controller.
