# SCENE 01 — THE EXTRACTION ROOM

Status: **First full erotic-scene design pass**

Purpose:

This is the first scene designed from the player’s tastes outward.

It exists to prove that BLACKSITE can take **one strong premise** and develop it deeply instead of jumping between shallow mechanics.

Core premise:

> Reina has been carrying several internal covert devices/balls for a long stretch of the mission. She reaches a private room where they can finally be removed. The Commander controls the release system and knows more about the hardware than she does. What should be a simple extraction turns into a long, humiliating struggle.

---

# 1. Preconditions

Before this scene begins:

- Reina has already carried the devices through several rooms.
- The player has seen her movement gradually worsen.
- She has insisted more than once that she can keep going.
- She has not been allowed to simply remove them whenever she wants.
- The player knows how many were inserted.
- Reina may **not** know the exact count.
- The Commander has access to the control interface.
- At least one device has been actively vibrating/oscillating for scanner evasion.
- Reina is tired enough that the extraction begins with real urgency.

This is important.

The scene starts **after anticipation has already accumulated**.

---

# 2. Entry

Reina gets into the room first.

She checks the door.

Locks it.

Immediately:

> “Unlock them.”

No greeting. No discussion.

If the Commander stalls:

> “Now.”

If he looks around the room first:

> “You have had the entire facility to sightsee.”

She is already near the end of her patience.

The player can:

- unlock immediately
- inspect the control terminal
- ask how bad it is
- tell her to wait
- tease her about how urgently she asked

These choices establish the Commander’s mood.

---

# 3. Reina tries to do it herself

Once the retention lock is released, Reina assumes she can handle the rest privately.

She orders the Commander to:

> “Turn around.”

The player can comply, half-comply, or refuse.

Even if he turns away, he can still hear:

- her breathing change
- clothing shifting
- the first failed attempt
- a frustrated pause
- another attempt

This section matters because Reina must first believe **she does not need him**.

After enough failure:

> “Don’t say anything.”

The Commander has not necessarily said anything.

That is how obvious the problem is.

---

# 4. First visible problem

Eventually Reina admits:

> “One is at the opening.”

But it will not pass.

This is the first long physical beat.

Do not immediately resolve it.

The scene can branch through:

### Let her continue

She keeps pushing.

Progress is slow.

The ball/device becomes partially visible.

Stops.

Moves another fraction.

Stops again.

Reina’s legs begin shaking.

If the player stares too long, she notices.

> “Are you enjoying the view?”

Possible responses:

- “Very much.”
- “Monitoring progress.”
- “Do you want me to stop looking?”
- say nothing

Her reaction depends on prior treatment.

### Give instructions

The Commander can tell her to:

- change posture
- relax
- push harder
- stop and breathe
- try again on a count
- let him inspect how stuck it is

Some instructions genuinely help.

Some are only technically useful.

Some are the Commander exploiting the situation.

### Offer help

Early offer:

> “No.”

Second offer:

> “I said no.”

Later offer, after prolonged failure:

Long silence.

> “…What exactly do you mean by help?”

That line is an escalation milestone.

---

# 5. Stuck-at-the-door phase

This is not a transition.

It is a major scene state.

The first ball is visibly trying to pass, but Reina cannot get it over the final point.

Desired ingredients:

- she pushes until her breathing breaks
- she stops because it hurts/overwhelms
- the object retreats slightly
- she gets angry because she lost progress
- another push returns it to the edge
- the Commander can comment on exactly how close it is
- Reina may demand that he stop narrating
- the Commander can offer to hold/assist
- Reina may insist on “one more try” repeatedly

A particularly good loop:

1. almost out
2. stops
3. Reina tries harder
4. loses control briefly
5. object slips back
6. Reina swears
7. Commander asks if she wants help now
8. she says no
9. repeat, worse

This is the kind of repetition BLACKSITE should not be afraid of.

---

# 6. Help threshold

Reina should not automatically accept help because a timer expired.

She reaches a **character threshold**.

Possible triggers:

- too many failed attempts
- legs too shaky to hold posture
- visible object slips back twice
- player points out that another device is pressing behind it
- she realizes she is wasting mission time
- she has a humiliating bodily accident
- the Commander deliberately waits for her to ask clearly

The player can make the moment easier or worse.

Gentle version:

> “Reina. Let me help.”

She closes her eyes.

> “…Fine.”

Crueler version:

> “Ask.”

Silence.

> “Commander.”

> “Ask properly.”

This should be available because the mod is for a player who enjoys the command/humiliation dynamic.

---

# 7. First successful removal

When the first device finally comes out, it should feel like a genuine event.

Not:

\`DEVICE_COUNT -= 1\`

Reina has been fighting for it.

The release gives her a burst of overwhelming relief and loss of control.

She may:

- collapse forward
- shake
- gasp
- lose her train of thought
- involuntarily vocalize
- squirt
- piss
- or have some combination depending on how hard the scene was pushed

The player may have chosen earlier variables that make bladder loss more likely.

If she pisses:

Do **not** cut away.

There is a silence afterward where both know exactly what happened.

Reina’s first instinct may be denial, anger, or simply staring at the floor.

The Commander can:

- say nothing
- tease her
- reassure her
- point out that the recording caught it
- remind her there are still more devices

That last one is the knife.

---

# 8. “There are more.”

Reina starts to recover.

She may reach for clothing.

The Commander checks the terminal.

Then:

> “Four remaining.”

Reina freezes.

This is a major emotional beat.

She may genuinely have believed there were only two or three.

Possible reaction:

> “…Four?”

The Commander confirms.

She counts mentally.

Realizes how long this is going to take.

That is the scene’s second beginning.

The player should feel:

**we are nowhere near finished.**

---

# 9. The later devices should not all behave the same

Each subsequent removal can have a different problem.

Example progression:

### Device two — faster but more intense

The path is already sensitized/stretched.

It moves more quickly.

Reina is less able to maintain control.

### Device three — stuck behind the second

Two devices bunch together internally.

The Commander has to alter the control signal.

Reina must hold still while the internal position changes.

### Device four — false finish

It comes out relatively cleanly.

Reina believes that was the last one.

The Commander knows it was not.

He can tell her immediately or let her begin dressing first.

### Device five — the bastard

The deepest one.

It has been active the longest.

It is the most difficult to move.

By now Reina is exhausted, messy, angry, embarrassed, and far less capable of pretending this is under control.

That creates natural escalation without inventing a new fetish every thirty seconds.

---

# 10. Dialogue escalation

Dialogue should change with Reina’s condition.

Early:

> “I can manage this.”

Mid:

> “Stop watching me like that.”

Later:

> “It’s stuck.”

Later:

> “I know you can see that.”

Help threshold:

> “…Come here.”

After bodily loss of control:

> “Do not say a word.”

Near the end:

> “Just get the rest out.”

This is the desired erosion of control.

---

# 11. Commander cruelty/opportunism options

The Commander can choose to:

- unlock all devices immediately
- unlock them one at a time
- delay release
- make Reina try alone longer
- make her ask for help
- provide useful posture advice
- provide suspiciously specific advice
- record the process
- move closer to inspect
- ask how many she thinks remain
- let her believe she is finished for a moment
- tell her the count immediately
- tease her after an accident
- help clean up
- refuse to let her dress until the count is confirmed
- reveal afterward that a faster extraction mode existed

Not all options need to be “optimal.”

Some exist because the player likes being mean.

---

# 12. Reina fights back

Reina should not only absorb humiliation.

She can:

- call out obvious stalling
- threaten retaliation after extraction
- refuse a clearly useless instruction
- deliberately stop responding to teasing
- demand the terminal
- grab the Commander if he gets too smug
- remember exact wording for later
- make the Commander help with cleanup
- later weaponize one of his own comments against him

This keeps her a character.

---

# 13. Aftermath

The scene is not complete when the final ball comes out.

Immediate aftermath may include:

- trembling legs
- difficulty standing normally
- clothing rearrangement
- wet/messy clothing or floor
- cleanup choice
- residual sensitivity
- anger toward Commander
- exhaustion
- relief
- terminal record
- camera footage
- recovered physical devices that still need to be carried out

The Commander can choose:

- take time to recover
- move immediately
- clean thoroughly
- do the minimum
- delete footage
- keep footage
- claim it is mission evidence
- hand Reina the recovered devices to carry externally now, which is insulting in a completely different way

The next scene must know which happened.

---

# 14. Later callbacks

Possible callbacks later in Glasshouse:

- Reina refuses a chair because sitting is uncomfortable.
- A guard asks why she is walking carefully.
- The recovered balls rattle inside a container at the worst possible moment.
- A terminal identifies their serial numbers.
- Reina sees the same device rack and immediately says “No.”
- The Commander finds the “rapid extraction” documentation later.
- Reina discovers that he could have shortened the first removal.
- A later mission proposes the same device family and Reina starts negotiating conditions **before** insertion.
- Eventually Reina herself suggests using them because they are tactically effective, creating the horrible normalization payoff.

---

# 15. What this scene requires from the game

This scene tells us what systems are actually worth building.

Required eventually:

- per-device object state, not one generic compromise meter
- visible remaining count
- individual device position/progress
- stuck/slip-back behavior
- posture states
- player-controlled release/lock state
- physical help state
- Reina refusal/acceptance thresholds
- layered dialogue state
- camera/recording state
- bladder state
- mess/cleanup aftermath
- locomotion consequences
- persistent evidence
- later callback flags

This is why BLACKSITE should be designed scene-first.

The scene tells us what the engine needs.
