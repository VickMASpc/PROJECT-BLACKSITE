# G1 — Reina Companion Prototype

Status: **ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT**

G1 contains no mission scripting and no adult-scene implementation.

It exists to make Reina herself usable.

## Active controls

- **H** — FOLLOW / WAIT
- **G** — COME HERE
- **J** — MOVE THERE at the crosshair
- **R** — manual recovery beside player
- **K** — toggle development HUD

## Current implementation

- real hierarchical `models/reina.bbmodel`
- WORLD-parented model, visible in first person
- field-model v1 silhouette and palette
- procedural idle breathing
- walk cycle
- arm/leg secondary motion
- head gaze toward the player
- FOLLOW
- WAIT
- COME HERE
- MOVE THERE
- manual recovery only; no routine teleport-follow
- local ground probing
- small obstacle sidestep attempts
- step/drop limits
- lost/blocked reporting
- crosshair look detection
- compact development HUD

## G1 exit criteria

G1 is not complete until hands-on testing confirms:

- model loads correctly
- orientation is correct
- walk direction matches movement
- follow distance feels natural
- stairs/slabs/basic uneven terrain are tolerable
- doorway behavior is not constantly annoying
- gaze looks natural rather than possessed
- COME HERE ends in a sensible position
- MOVE THERE is predictable
- WAIT truly stays put
- manual recovery works
- no first-person duplicate-head problem
- ten minutes of ordinary walking around feels like a companion rather than a floating prop

Any failure in those areas is a G1 bug, not something to hide behind future content.
